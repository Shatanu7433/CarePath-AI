#  CarePath AI

### From Fragmented Records to a Clear Patient Journey.

CarePath AI is a mobile-first **Consultation Readiness & Patient Journey Management Platform** designed for maternal and women's healthcare teams.

It helps doctors and care teams organize existing patient information, review a chronological patient journey, identify missing or incomplete records, and prepare for upcoming consultations — all from one simple interface.

> **CarePath AI organizes healthcare information. Clinical decisions remain with healthcare professionals.**

---

##  Problem

Women receiving maternal and gynecological care may have information distributed across:

- Previous consultation notes
- Prescriptions
- Laboratory reports
- Imaging/ultrasound reports
- Referral documents
- Hospital records
- Patient questionnaires
- Follow-up records

This fragmented information can make it difficult for care teams to quickly reconstruct a patient's journey before a consultation.

### The core problem:

> **The information exists, but the complete patient journey is not always immediately accessible in one place.**

---

##  Our Solution

CarePath AI transforms fragmented healthcare records into a structured and chronological patient journey.

The platform allows care teams to:

- View patient history chronologically
- Organize healthcare documents
- Search existing records
- Review patient-submitted information
- Identify missing or incomplete administrative information
- Track follow-up activities
- Prepare for upcoming consultations

---

##  Key Features

###  Doctor Dashboard
A centralized dashboard showing:

- Today's consultations
- Patients
- Pending documents
- Follow-ups
- Questionnaires

###  Patient Journey

A chronological timeline showing:

- Consultations
- Uploaded documents
- Patient questionnaires
- Follow-up activities
- Upcoming appointments

###  Consultation Preparation

Before a consultation, the care team can review:

- Available patient information
- Previous consultation records
- Available documents
- Questionnaire completion
- Missing/incomplete information
- Recent recorded activities

###  Document Management

- Upload documents
- Categorize documents
- Search records
- View source documents
- Access documents from the patient journey

### Patient Questionnaire

Patients can provide information before their appointment through structured questionnaires.

###  Follow-Up Tracking

Care teams can track administrative follow-ups such as:

- Requested documents
- Questionnaires
- Communication tasks
- Record updates
